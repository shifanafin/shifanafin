Overview

AI-powered automotive workshop management

Comet Autos digitizes the day-to-day operations of an automotive workshop — from customer and vehicle intake through job cards, parts and invoicing — and layers Gemini-powered AI directly into the workflow rather than bolting a chatbot on the side.

Document Intelligence — a document image goes through Gemini's multimodal model, comes back as structured JSON, gets validated by the application, and lands in form data feeding the business workflow.

Document Image→ Gemini Vision / Multimodal→ Structured JSON→ Application Validation→ Form Data→ Business Workflow

AI Service Advisor — a customer complaint plus vehicle context (make, model, year, mileage) goes to Gemini, which returns possible causes, an inspection checklist and technician tasks.

AI-assisted service guidance for the technician — not autonomous vehicle diagnosis.
Complaint + Vehicle Context→ Gemini→ Possible Causes→ Inspection Checklist→ Technician Tasks

What's next
